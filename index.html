Paddock to Plate

Our wagyu cattle are free to roam in open paddocks with full access to fresh water and shade from native trees.

Slow grown, we use no Bovaer, growth promotors or hormones for a natural, healthy end product.

Our wagyu are grass fed and finished, taking longer to mature as a result, but good results are worth the wait.

Grass fed beef is widely regarded as a healthier alternative to grain fed beef, as it is 100% naturally raised with no GMO contaminated feed.

Reared sustainably in the Southern Highlands, our beef accumulates very few food miles as it is born and raised in Burradoo, processed in Picton and locally butchered.
